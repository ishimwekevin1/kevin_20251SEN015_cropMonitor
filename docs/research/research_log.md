## Dataset inspection notes
The supplied NISR archive contains four survey tables for each of three seasons (12 CSVs total). Crop_Production row counts: Season A 39,330; Season B 35,584; Season C 1,949; total 76,863. The inspected production files have 96, 98 and 97 columns respectively. Season is derived from the source filename because it is not present as a dedicated field in those CSVs. The data dictionary workbook was reported to contain 774 metadata rows.

## Source and data integrity
NISR's terms of use state that NISR data and analysis are generally released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), unless a particular item specifies different terms. Attribute NISR and preserve data integrity: https://www.statistics.gov.rw/terms-use.

The data are survey observations, not a census. Keep source rows traceable. Do not silently recode 9999 or delete negative values without checking the questionnaire. Farmer-crop utilization/loss fields can repeat across plot rows; aggregate them at the correct grain. Do not report national weighted estimates until NISR's weighting methodology is verified.

**Student preparation:** Read the full text of the two research papers and the MINAGRI report before the defence. The links and bibliographic metadata have been checked, but the student should be able to explain the sources personally.
